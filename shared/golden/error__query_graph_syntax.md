### ❌ Invalid argument
Cannot parse the query. Grammar: `<relation>[*<depth>] of <name> [in <glob>]`, relations: callers, callees, bases, derived, overrides, members, includers, includes, definitions; or `entry-points [in <glob>]`. Examples: `callers*2 of process_packet`, `derived of Transport`, `includers*2 of packet.hpp in src/*`, `entry-points`.
